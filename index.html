AI targeting suggestions
High
Add a 1–2% Lookalike Audience from your top purchasers (180 days)
Your 25–34 segment converts 2.4× better than average. A LAL finds similar high-intent profiles at scale without manual interest stacking.
High
Enable Advantage+ Audience on your best-performing ad set
Once you have 300+ conversion events, Meta's algorithm outperforms manual interest targeting. Advantage+ expands reach automatically.
Medium
Exclude last 30-day purchasers with a Custom Audience
Without exclusions you're paying to retarget people who already converted. This alone can reduce wasted spend by 10–20%.
Medium
Test a Reels-only placement with a 15-second creative
Reels CPM is 30–40% cheaper than Feed. If your Reels CTR is above 1.5%, you have untapped cheap reach.
Low
Broaden age targeting to 18–54 and let Meta optimize delivery
Restricting age raises CPM. Broad targeting + strong creative often beats narrow targeting + weak creative.
