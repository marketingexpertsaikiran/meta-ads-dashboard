A "persona" = a specific audience segment you target (e.g. "Traders", "Investors", "Beginners"). By naming your ad sets with the persona name (e.g. "Traders_LAL_1pct"), you can filter your Meta insights by persona and compare ROAS, CPA and CTR across each group.

Common Causes & Fixes
Cause 1
Budget fragmentation — too many ad sets
Meta needs 50 conversions per week per ad set to exit learning. If you have 8 ad sets on a ₹10,000/day budget = ₹1,250/ad set/day. Unless your CPA is under ₹178, you'll never exit learning. Fix: Consolidate to 2–3 ad sets max.
Cause 2
Budget too low relative to your CPA
Rule: Daily budget should be at least 5× your CPA. If CPA = ₹500, you need ₹2,500/day minimum per ad set. Below this, Meta can't collect enough signal data. Fix: Increase budget or switch optimization event to something that fires more often (e.g. "Add to Cart" instead of "Purchase").
Cause 3
Frequent edits keep resetting learning
Every significant change (budget >20%, bid, targeting, creative) resets the learning phase clock back to 0. Fix: Make changes in max 20% increments, wait 3–4 days between changes.
Cause 4
Audience too narrow for algorithm to learn
Under 100K audience = Meta can't find variation patterns. Fix: Expand to 500K+ or enable Advantage+ Audience.
