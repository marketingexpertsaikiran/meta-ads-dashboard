Common Learning Phase Issues
Crit
Too many ad sets split budget (fragmentation)
Each ad set needs 50 conversions/week to exit learning. 8 ad sets at $50/day = $6.25/day each — nearly impossible to exit. Consolidate to 2–3 ad sets.
High
Budget below minimum threshold
Meta needs ~50× your CPA as daily budget to exit learning. If CPA = $30, you need $1,500/day minimum. Scale or switch to cost cap.
Med
Audience too narrow for algorithm to learn
Under 100K audience = limited data variety. Algorithm can't find patterns. Expand to 500K+ or switch to Advantage+ audience.
Low
Too many edits restarting learning
Every significant edit (budget, bid, targeting) resets the learning phase. Make changes infrequently and in under 20% increments.
