Common Learning Phase Issues
Crit
Budget fragmentation across too many ad sets
Each ad set needs 50 conversions/week to exit learning. 8 ad sets at $50/day = $6.25 each — nearly impossible. Consolidate to 2–3 ad sets.
High
Budget below minimum threshold
Meta needs ~50× your CPA as daily budget to exit learning. If CPA = ₹2,000, you need ₹100,000/day. Use cost cap bidding instead.
Med
Audience too narrow — algorithm can't learn patterns
Under 100K audience = limited signal variety. Expand to 500K+ or switch to Advantage+ audience for faster exit from learning.
Low
Frequent edits resetting the learning clock
Every significant edit (bid, budget, targeting) resets learning. Make changes less than 20% at a time and wait 7 days between.
